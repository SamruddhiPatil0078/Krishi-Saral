Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (Sample Scheme for Demo)

Eligible beneficiaries shall be entitled to financial assistance subject to fulfillment of prescribed eligibility criteria. The scheme is applicable to small and marginal farmer families who own cultivable land, subject to the exclusion criteria laid down by the State Government.

Under this scheme, an amount of Rs. 6000 per year shall be provided to each eligible farmer family, in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 each, directly transferred to the bank account of the beneficiary.

Applicants must furnish the following documents at the time of registration: Aadhaar card, bank account passbook, and land record (7/12 extract) in the name of the applicant.

Farmers who wish to apply shall be required to register online through the official portal, or may submit the application offline at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or the Block Agriculture Office.

The last date for submission of applications for the current financial year is 31st March 2024. Applications received after the due date shall not be considered for the current cycle and shall be processed in the subsequent cycle, subject to availability of funds.

Provided that any farmer family in which one or more members is a former or present holder of a constitutional post shall not be eligible for benefits under this scheme. It is further clarified that the benefit shall be limited to one installment per eligible family per year, and any false declaration shall lead to recovery of the disbursed amount along with applicable penalty.
